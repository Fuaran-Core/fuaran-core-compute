# Compute benchmarks — 2026-11-11, AMD Ryzen 9 9950X, Phase 631 (the pinned special functions)

Phase 631 pins the gamma function and its logarithm, the beta function and its logarithm, the regularized
incomplete gamma and beta functions, the inverse of the regularized incomplete beta, and the polygamma functions
(`Fuaran.Compute.Elementary`'s `Special`, `DECISIONS.md` D78). This file records the measurement the decision
rests on: each function's error against a fifty-digit reference, its cost per value on .NET and under node, and
the candidates each choice was made between.

## How it was taken

- Machine: AMD Ryzen 9 9950X (16 cores), Windows 11; .NET SDK 10.0.203 (Release, x64), node v22.23.4
  (Fable 5.0.0, the repository's tool manifest).
- Harness: the node leg's timing program (`benchmarks/Fuaran.Core.Compute.Benchmarks/Node`), family `special`
  (`../SpecialBench.fs`), the same program on both hosts:
  - .NET: `dotnet run -c Release --project benchmarks/Fuaran.Core.Compute.Benchmarks/Node -- 5 special > dotnet.txt`
  - node: `pwsh benchmarks/Fuaran.Core.Compute.Benchmarks/Node/run-node.ps1 -FableFrom . -Runs 5 -Only special > node.txt`
  - the check: the lines between `BEGIN special-samples` and `END special-samples`, through
    `python tests/Fuaran.Core.Compute.Tests/SpecialReference/special_reference.py check`
- The sample is a seeded draw at 631 through the pinned Park–Miller generator and the pinned `exp`, `ln` and
  `pow`, so it is the same 29,563 inputs on every host. Per function:
  - `gammaLn`: 1,500 uniform over `(0, 3.5]`; either side of the zeros at one and two down to the last place;
    200 log-uniform over `[1e-300, 1e-3]`; 1,000 uniform over `[2.5, 30]`; 500 log-uniform over `[30, 1e300]`;
    and, scored apart as the reflection, 1,000 uniform over `(−30, 0)` and 200 log-uniform over `[−1e6, −30]`.
  - `gamma`: 2,000 uniform over `(0, 171.6)`; 300 log-uniform over `[1e-300, 1]`; every half-integer from `−19.5`
    to `170.5`; every integer from `24` to `171`; and, as the reflection, 1,000 uniform over `(−170, 0)`.
  - `betaLn`, `beta`: 1,500 pairs log-uniform over `[1e-6, 1e6]²`, 1,500 over `[1e-3, 85]²`, and a grid of
    integers to `39`.
  - `regularizedGammaP`, `regularizedGammaQ`: `a` log-uniform over `[1e-10, 1)` (600), `[1, 10)` (600),
    `[10, 1e3)` (500) and `[1e3, 3e5)` (60); `x` within six or seven standard deviations of `a` seven draws in
    ten, across four and a half decades round it otherwise.
  - `regularizedBeta`: both parameters log-uniform over `[1e-3, 1)` (500), `[1e-2, 10)` (500), `[10, 1e3)` (400)
    and `[1e3, 2e4)` (60), and 200 with `a` in `[1e-2, 5)` against `b` in `[50, 1e4)`; `x` within six standard
    deviations of the mean six draws in ten, uniform two in ten, within twelve decades of an end otherwise.
  - `inverseRegularizedBeta`: 1,200 with both parameters log-uniform over `[0.05, 1e3)`; `p` uniform six draws in
    ten, within twelve decades of `0` or of `1` otherwise.
  - `digamma`: 1,500 uniform over `(0, 2.2]`; either side of the root near `1.4616` down to the last place; 100
    log-uniform over `[1e-300, 1e-2]`; 1,500 uniform over `[2, 40]`; 400 log-uniform over `[40, 1e300]`; and, as
    the reflection, 1,000 uniform over `(−40, 0)`.
  - `trigamma` (800) and `polygamma` at orders `2` to `8` (400 each), `12` and `20` (200 each): half log-uniform
    over `[1e-3, 1e3]`, half uniform up to twice the order's shift threshold.
- Each case: a warm-up of at least half a second, then the median of 5 samples, each enough passes to span fifty
  milliseconds (Phase 593's timer, unchanged).
- The reference is mpmath 1.4.1 at fifty digits, rounded once to the nearest double; the distance is in units in
  the last place of that double. An input whose true value is below `1e-290` in magnitude, or that mpmath cannot
  take, is not scored (the last column). The script's header says how each function is spelled.
- The two captures' sample lines are compared byte for byte: that is the byte-identity claim. They were identical,
  all 29,563.

| beta | 3116 | 666 | 126 | 5.5 | 34 | 144 |

Where each maximum comes from:

- **A zero the function crosses.** `gammaLn` below zero, `digamma` below zero and `betaLn` each pass through zero
  where two terms of ordinary size cancel (`ln |Γ|` between the poles, `ψ` between the poles, `ln B` where `B` is
  one). The absolute error there is a few units in the last place of the terms; relative to an answer near zero it
  is unbounded, and the maxima above are those inputs. On the positive axis `gammaLn` has its zeros at one and two
  and `digamma` its root near `1.4616` as factors of a kernel, and both stay within five units through them.
- **A large exponent.** `beta` beyond `a + b = 170`, and the incomplete functions in a deep tail, are `exp` of an
  exponent of magnitude `E`, whose rounding costs about `2E` units: `beta`'s 666 is at `B ≈ 1e-285`, and the
  incomplete functions' four-figure maxima are at values below `1e-200`. The last error column is the same sample
  held to values no smaller than `1e-10`.
- **A long series.** `regularizedGammaP` at `a` in the tens of thousands with `x` near `a` sums thousands of terms
  by a recurrence, and each term carries the rounding of those before it: 40 units at `a = 1e5`, 182 at `a = 1e6`
  (the vectors `gammaP-large-a-x-is-a` and `gammaP-very-large-a-below`).
- **Conditioning.** The inverse's answer moves by `1 / a` of the function's relative error where `x` is small: its
  477 is at `a = 0.056`.

For scale, the pinned `exp` is 18 ns and `pow` 204 ns a value on .NET (Phase 593's measurement, another machine).
No candidate's cost was out of proportion to what it computes. One cost was found and removed during the
measurement: with the stopping test in a small private function, each series term cost about 19 ns on .NET against
4 ns under node, because the call to `StoppingRule.stops` sat in tail position and the tail call kept the loop from
the optimising tier. The function is `inline` now; `regularizedGammaP` fell from 806 ns to 238 ns a value on .NET
and nothing else moved.

## The candidates

The choices between algorithms were made on a prototype of each candidate in plain doubles (Python, the host's
`exp`, `ln` and `pow` standing in for the pinned ones, which are within a unit of them), against the same
reference over samples of the shapes above. The shipped F# was then measured on its own, as the tables record. The
prototype's figures, largest error in units in the last place:

| choice | candidates | figures |
|---|---|---|
| `gammaLn` over `(0, 3.5]` | Lanczos (`g = 7`, nine terms); Stirling from `10` after shifting up; the kernel | Lanczos `1e16` (it loses the zeros at one and two); shift-up `1e17` (the same); kernel `3.7` |
| `gammaLn` over `[2.5, 30]` | Lanczos; shift-up Stirling; kernel below a threshold `T` and Stirling with `K` terms above | Lanczos `46`; shift-up `50`; `T = 6, K = 9`: `2.6`; `T = 8, K = 7`: `1.9`; `T = 10, K = 6`: `1.9`. `T = 10, K = 8` ships |
| the kernel's degree | 11, 13, 15, 17 | `4.9e4`, `698`, `13.8`, `4.6`; degree 17 ships (19 to 23 measure the same) |
| `gamma` over `(0, 171.6)` | `exp (gammaLn x)`; the kernel with the recurrence's product below `10` and Stirling's formula over the pinned `pow` above | `exp (gammaLn)`: `1160` (99th percentile `793`); the second: `4.4` |
| `beta` with arguments to `85` | `exp (betaLn)`; `Γ(a) Γ(b) / Γ(a + b)` where `a + b ≤ 170` | `154`; `9.5` (with the rounding of `a + b` restored; without it `120`) |
| `betaLn` | three `gammaLn` calls; Stirling arms from `10` | `3e7`; `5.7` away from the zero of `ln B` |
| `digamma` over `(0, 2.2]` | shifting up to `10` and the asymptotic series (the shard's one algorithm); the kernel | shift-up: `111` away from the root, unbounded at it; kernel: `2.0` |
| the digamma kernel's degree | 15, 17, 19, 21, 23 | `6200`, `186`, `6.8`, `3.3`, `2.0`; degree 23 ships |
| `digamma`'s series | threshold `6`, `8`, `10`, `12`; `5` to `9` terms | threshold `8`, `7` terms: `3.6`; threshold `10`, `6` terms: `2.5`. Threshold `10`, `7` terms ships |
| `polygamma`'s threshold and length, orders `1` to `20` | threshold `8` to `40`; `6`, `8`, `10`, `12` terms | with `10` terms the error settles (below about `8` units, `16` at order `20`) at a threshold of `10` for orders `1` to `3`, `12` at `4`, `16` at `6` and `8`, `20` at `12`, `28` at `20`; the rule `10 + n` ships |
| the incomplete gamma prefix, `a` in `[10, 1e3)` and `[1e3, 3e5)` | `exp (a ln x − x − gammaLn a)`; the form in `μ − ln (1 + μ)` | `7.6e3` and `1e6`; `743` (a deep tail) and `108` |
