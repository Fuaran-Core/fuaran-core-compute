# Compute benchmarks — 2026-11-12, AMD Ryzen 9 9950X, Phase 636 (the incomplete beta function at a large parameter, and its complement)

Phase 636 repairs `Special.regularizedBeta` where one parameter is large, publishes
`Special.regularizedBetaComplement` and `Special.inverseRegularizedBetaComplement`, and makes the t's and the F's forms
direct reads of them (`DECISIONS.md` D81). This file records the measurement the decision rests on: the premise, the
function's error against a fifty-digit reference before and after by range, the candidates each choice was made
between, the re-run of Phase 631's and Phase 618's samples, and the costs.

## How it was taken

- Machine: AMD Ryzen 9 9950X (16 cores), Windows 11; .NET SDK 10.0.203 (Release, x64), node v22.23.4 (Fable 5.0.0,
  the repository's tool manifest). The machine was not quiet during the timings.
- Harness: the node leg's timing program (`benchmarks/Fuaran.Core.Compute.Benchmarks/Node`), the same program on
  both hosts, three families:
  - `beta-corner` (`../BetaCornerBench.fs`, new): seeded at 636;
  - `distributions` (`../DistributionsBench.fs`): Phase 618's sample, every input the input it was, with the forms
    Phase 618 shipped added as candidates (`d79`) and 1,200 F inputs with a degree of freedom in `[4e4, 1e6)` drawn
    after everything else;
  - `special` (`../SpecialBench.fs`): Phase 631's sample, unchanged.
  - .NET: `dotnet run -c Release --project benchmarks/Fuaran.Core.Compute.Benchmarks/Node -- 5 beta-corner > dotnet.txt`
  - node: `pwsh benchmarks/Fuaran.Core.Compute.Benchmarks/Node/run-node.ps1 -FableFrom . -Runs 5 -Only beta-corner > node.txt`
  - the check: the lines between `BEGIN <family>-samples` and `END <family>-samples`, through
    `python tests/Fuaran.Core.Compute.Tests/SpecialReference/special_reference.py check` (the distributions' through
    `DistributionsReference/distributions_reference.py check`).
- The `beta-corner` sample, per function (`regularizedBeta`, `regularizedBetaComplement`, and the two inverses with a
  probability drawn as Phase 618 drew one):
  - `corner`: `(a, b, 1 − c / a)`, `a` the large parameter, `b` the small one, `c` log-uniform over `[1e-3, 30)`; five
    ranges of 300: `a` in `[128, 2e4)` with `b` in `[0.05, 1)` and in `[1, 10)`; `a` in `[128, 1e6)` with `b` in
    `[1e-6, 0.05)`; `a` in `[2e4, 1e6)` with `b` in `[0.05, 1)` and in `[1, 10)`.
  - `mirror`: the same points read from the other end, `(b, a, c / a)`.
  - `both-large`: 120 with both parameters log-uniform over `[1e3, 1e6)` and `x` within six standard deviations of
    the mean.
  - `by-sum`: 240 a range with `a + b` log-uniform from one power of two to the next, `16` to `2048`; half with one
    parameter in `[0.05, min (10, (a + b) / 4))`, half with both of a size; `x` within six standard deviations of the
    mean.
  - `general`: Phase 631's draws (`betaTriples`, `inverseSample`), for the two new functions.
- "Before" is the same program over the function as Phase 631 left it: the threshold of D81 item 2 set above every
  double in a working copy, which restores the old fraction, the old restoration and Gauss's series at every sum
  (the lines of the `special` family from that build are byte-identical to the build before the phase). The
  complement's "before" is therefore the upper value the old function computed inside itself and published nowhere.
- The reference is mpmath 1.4.1 at fifty digits, rounded once to the nearest double; a distance is in units in the
  last place of that double. An inverse's reference is the root nearest the package's answer. An input whose true
  value is below `1e-290`, or whose answer is zero or one where the reference rounds there too, is not scored.
- The two hosts' sample lines were compared byte for byte and were identical: 17,960 of `beta-corner`, 35,480 of
  `distributions`, 29,563 of `special`.

## The premise

`I_x(a, ½)` at `x = 2a / (2a + 3.84)`, the t's tail at a `t²` of `3.84`, in units in the last place from the reference:

| `a` | 50 | 500 | 5,000 | 20,000 | 50,000 | 500,000 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| before | 35 | 247 | 3,660 | 1,420 | 11,900 | 176,900 |
| after | 35 | 5 | 25 | 3 | 7 | 20 |

At `a = 50` the parameters sum below the threshold and the function is the one it was. Phase 618 reported the same
growth in units of `2⁻⁵²` (260, 6,400 and 200,000 at 500, 20,000 and 500,000); the figure moves with the input, and
the cause is one subtraction: the first denominator of the continued fraction, `1 − (a + b) x / (a + 1)`, is
`λ / (a + 1)` with `λ = a + 1 − (a + b) x` of order one there.

## The fraction's form, on a prototype

Four forms in host floats, before the package was touched (60 inputs a cell, `b` in `[0.05, 1)`, the argument within
`30 / a` of one; largest error of the directly computed member of the pair):

| form | `a` in `[50, 500)` | `[500, 2e4)` | `[2e4, 1e6)` |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Abramowitz and Stegun 26.5.8, as shipped by Phase 631 | 109 | 1,254 | 223,800 |
| the same, its first denominator formed from `λ` | 46 | 1,351 | 253,000 |
| its even part (DiDonato and Morris), `λ` in plain doubles | 87 | 722 | 72,740 |
| its even part, `λ` with the rounding errors of its sum and product | 26 | 14 | 28 |

Repairing the first denominator alone does nothing: every odd partial quotient is within `1 / a` of minus one and is
added to one. The even part has no such sum, and then `λ` is the whole of it.

## The reference

`special_reference.py` computed the incomplete beta function by `betainc`, and for both parameters at least ten
summing to two thousand or more by a quadrature of the density cut at the standard deviation's scale. Against
`betainc` at seventy digits that quadrature is exact near the mean and `1.4e-13` of the value out 13.5 standard
deviations into a tail, `1.2e-4` at 22.9 and `1.2e-5` at 28.4. It is replaced, for every sum of two thousand or
more, by Abramowitz and Stegun's fraction 26.5.8 evaluated from its tail at eighty digits, the count of partial
quotients doubled until two evaluations agree to `1e-45`:

- against `betainc` at ninety digits, both parameters in `[1e3, 6e3)`, out to thirty standard deviations either
  side, the function and its complement: `6.6e-52` at worst over 30 inputs;
- against the `betainc` references of this phase's own corner sample (one parameter below ten, the other up to a
  million): `2.3e-51` at worst over 1,241 inputs;
- every reference the vectors carried re-derives to the same bits: 182 of `SpecialVectors.references` and 143 of
  `DistributionsVectors.references`.

Phase 631's table was taken against the quadrature; the re-run above is against the fraction.
